Space-Mining-Game — SRX integration drop-in
==========================================
1. Copy these files into your Space-Mining-Game repo root (overwrite):
   - game.js
   - index.html
   - three-renderer.js
   - srx-bridge.js
   - README.md

2. Set your exchange URL in game.js CFG.srx.baseUrl
   (or window.SRX_CONFIG.baseUrl) to your Render SRX deploy.

3. Open index.html / serve the folder and Launch Mission.
   Top-right panel = live prices + Open Exchange link.
   Docking & selling ore posts haul to the exchange account.

Repo: https://github.com/Codesurfing10/Space-Mining-Game
